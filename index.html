Chapter one

Where it all began

November 9, 2002

Our story began long before we became sweethearts, Mommy. Before we became husband and wife, or even dreamed of becoming Mommy and Daddy, we were elementary classmates.

Grade 4 to Grade 6. We shared the same classroom, never knowing that the classmate sitting nearby would someday become the love of my life.

The PUP entrance exam. When you took the entrance exam for Polytechnic University of the Philippines, Bataan Branch, I took it too because I wanted to study wherever you would be. I passed. You deliberately failed because you didn’t want to study there. Just like that, my clever plan completely failed. 😂

Our separate high-school days. We went to different schools and lived separate lives for a while. But during our fourth year, I first noticed you passing by our house on your way to school, and from then on I started looking forward to seeing you every morning, waiting just to have a little time to talk to you before you continued on your way. When I noticed that you had changed your route and stopped passing by our house, I knew I could not just keep waiting for another chance, so I finally gathered the courage to go to your house and formally court you.

The competition. I wasn’t your only suitor. There were two of us hoping you would choose one of us. I waited, kept trying, and continued showing you how I felt.

November 9, 2002. You finally accepted my love. From that day on, our journey truly began.

From elementary classmates, to two teenagers who found each other again, to Mommy and Daddy… I’m still thankful that you chose me. ♥

Since 2002

Chapter two

The road to “I do”

2002 → September 20, 2008

Before we could stand before everyone as husband and wife, Mommy, we first had to protect a love that almost no one was allowed to know about.

Ours was a love that learned how to wait, travel, persevere, and keep its promises. ♥

Our secret. Your parents did not allow you to have a boyfriend until you graduated from college, so for years we kept our relationship hidden from them. It was not always easy, but we found a way to stay together.

Every Friday. You studied at Tomas del Rosario College in Balanga City, while home was more than 40 kilometers away in Mariveles. Every Friday, when you were coming home for the weekend, I would fetch you from school. That long road became part of our love story, one trip at a time and one weekend at a time.

The one time I let you go. There was a time when we broke up. Leaving you was a mistake I made only once, because I realized that the life I wanted was still the life with you in it. I returned, and we chose to keep going together.

When the secret came out. Eventually, your father learned about us. You stood up for me and defended our relationship. I also faced him myself and promised that our love would not destroy your future. We would both finish college and take our responsibilities seriously.

A promise kept. We did what we said we would do. We persevered through the distance, the secrecy, and all the difficulties. We both graduated from college, proving that loving each other did not have to mean giving up our dreams.

Our sudden wedding. By then, our relationship had grown deeper and more intimate. When your period was delayed, a visit to the OB-GYN brought the truth into the open. Our parents knew that marriage was where our journey was already heading, so everything happened sooner and more suddenly than we had expected.

September 20, 2008. The wedding may have come together quickly, but the love behind it was not sudden at all. It had already survived six years of waiting, traveling, hiding, mistakes, forgiveness, courage, and promises kept. That was the day our secret love finally became our forever. ♥

Chapter three

Then we became Mommy & Daddy

November 23, 2009

We thought we would spend a little more time simply enjoying married life, Mommy. But love had another beautiful chapter waiting for us.

This is how the two of us became three. ♥

Just the two of us for a little while. After our wedding, we planned to enjoy being newlyweds, have fun, make memories, and simply learn how to be husband and wife together.

Waiting for a grandchild. Your parents were already excited to become grandparents. You were their only daughter and, among you and your two brothers, you were the only one married at the time. Eventually, we gave in to their loving encouragement. You stopped taking your contraceptive pills, and together we began hoping for a baby.

Our Baguio memory. A few months after the wedding, we traveled to Baguio City with my officemates from Santa Rosa, Laguna. I have always believed that the story of our first baby began there, among the cool air and the memories we made in Baguio.

A secret already growing. Later, we visited Corregidor with your parents. They wanted you to touch the fertility statue so that we could finally have a baby. What they did not know was that you were already pregnant. When we eventually told them, their happiness was impossible to hide.

A difficult journey. Carrying our first baby was not easy for you. I was working far away in Santa Rosa, Laguna, and there were checkups when I could not be beside you. Your mother stepped in, cared for you, and accompanied you whenever I was away. I will always be grateful that she was there when I could not be.

The checkup that changed everything. In November, you went for what was supposed to be a scheduled checkup. We had hoped for a normal delivery, but the doctors discovered that the umbilical cord was wrapped around our baby’s neck and that his heartbeat had become faint. They decided that an emergency Caesarean section was the safest way to protect both of you.

I came as quickly as I could. The moment the news reached me, I rushed to you. When I arrived, I found you lightheaded and groggy from the anesthesia. You were safe, and our baby was safe. I held you close and thanked God for bringing both of you through it.

And then there was JM. On November 23, 2009, our marriage became a family. You became Mommy, I became Daddy, and the little boy we had hoped and prayed for changed our world forever. ♥

Chapter four

Our family grew again

January 20, 2015

For almost five years, it was the three of us. We filled those years with little adventures, happy memories, and the joy of watching JM grow.

Then JM said yes to becoming a big brother. ♥

Our adventures as three. After JM was born, we enjoyed life as a small family. We went places, shared new experiences, and made memories with just the three of us.

A question for JM. We asked him if he wanted a sibling, and he said yes. We had always imagined having two children, one boy and one girl. Since we already had our little boy, we hoped the next baby would be a girl.

Trying one more time. Before JM turned five, we decided that we were ready for another baby. We were excited to grow our family, and we quietly wished that this time we would have a daughter.

Another baby boy. During the fifth month ultrasound, we learned that our baby was another boy. Our plan for one boy and one girl changed, but the love waiting for him was already there.

A difficult decision. While you were pregnant, we talked about whether we might try once more for a girl someday. During your final checkup, however, the doctors decided that you needed another Caesarean operation.

Choosing your safety. Since this would be your second Caesarean operation, we decided that you would also undergo ligation during the delivery. It was not an easy decision. I agreed because your health and safety mattered more to me than trying again for a daughter.

And then there were four. We waited through the operation, and on January 20, 2015, Ron Ron came into our lives. Our family became louder, busier, happier, and complete in a way that was different from what we first imagined, but every bit as beautiful.

The name Ron Ron. His nickname had already been part of our family before he was born. Ron Ron was the name of JM’s imaginary friend. The little friend from his imagination became the name of the real little brother he had asked for. ♥

All the years between

Little moments. Our whole life.

Not every beautiful memory is a big occasion. Sometimes it is a trip, a laugh, a silly picture, or simply all of us together. These ordinary moments became the life I treasure most.

The family I am thankful for. Mommy, you, JM, and Ron Ron are the greatest blessings in my life. When I look at our family, I see every sacrifice, every laugh, every hard day we survived, and every reason I have to keep becoming a better man. Thank you for helping me build a home filled with love and memories that I will always treasure.

I know I hurt you. There was a time when I did something wrong that almost caused our family to crumble. I know my actions caused pain, damaged your trust, and put everything we built at risk. I had my reasons at the time, but those reasons will never be enough to justify what I did. I accept that it was my mistake, and I am truly sorry for the hurt I brought into our home.

Thank you for giving me another chance. You did not owe me that chance, Mommy, but you still allowed me the opportunity to face what I had done, learn from it, and work on myself. I will never take that gift lightly. Your decision to stay was not permission for me to forget. It became a reason for me to remember every day that love also requires honesty, patience, responsibility, and change.

We chose each other again. We have faced hurdles that tested our marriage and our family. There were moments when giving up might have felt easier, but we chose to stay. We chose to keep talking, keep trying, and keep finding our way back to each other. I am very thankful that after everything, it is still us.

My promise to you and our boys. I will continue doing my best to become a better husband to you and a better father to JM and Ron Ron. I know that promises must be shown through actions, not only spoken on special days. I want to keep improving in the quiet, ordinary moments and prove through the way I love all of you that our family is worth protecting every single day.

Thank you for choosing our family, Mommy. Thank you for choosing us. I am happy that through every joy and every hurdle, we did not give up on the life we made together. ♥

Happy 18th Anniversary, Mommy.

18 years. Ang tagal na rin pala natin. Ang dami na nating pinagdaanan, masaya, mahirap, magulo, tahimik, minsan sobrang saya, minsan sobrang sakit. Pero sa lahat ng nangyari, sobrang thankful pa rin ako na ikaw ang kasama ko sa buhay.

Thank you for being my wife. Thank you for the family that you gave me. Thank you sa mga anak natin. Sila ang isa sa pinakamalaking dahilan kung bakit sobrang blessed ako sa buhay ko. Seeing our family, seeing our kids grow, knowing na ikaw ang nanay nila, sobrang laking bagay nun sa akin.

Alam ko hindi ko palaging nasasabi, at minsan hindi ko rin napapakita, pero naaappreciate ko lahat ng ginagawa mo. Lahat ng effort mo, pagod mo, pag-aalaga mo sa amin, pag-intindi mo, pati yung mga bagay na ginagawa mo kahit pagod ka na. Nakikita ko yun. Baka hindi ko lang nasasabi enough, pero sobrang thankful ako sa lahat.

I also want to say sorry.

Sorry sa mga panahon na naging kulang ako. Sorry sa mga pagkakataon na napaiyak kita, nasaktan kita, o naparamdam ko sayo na hindi kita napapahalagahan. Sorry sa lahat ng ginawa ko na naging dahilan para masaktan ka. Alam kong may mga bagay na hindi na mababawi, at alam kong may mga pagkakataon na hindi mo deserve yung mga sakit na naibigay ko sayo.

I know I’m not a perfect husband. Marami akong naging pagkukulang at marami pa rin akong kailangang ayusin sa sarili ko. Pero gusto kong malaman mo na mahal na mahal kita.

Mahal ko kung sino ka. Yung pagiging makulit mo, playful mo, pagiging moody mo, pati yung pagiging toyoin mo minsan. Minsan nakakainis, minsan nakakatawa, minsan hindi ko alam kung saan ako lulugar, pero mahal ko lahat yun kasi parte yun ng ikaw.

Thank you for the happiness you bring into my life. Thank you sa mga tawanan natin, sa mga simpleng kwentuhan, sa mga memories natin, pati sa mga araw na wala naman tayong ginagawa pero masaya ako dahil kasama kita.

Thank you for making our family what it is today.

After 18 years, ikaw pa rin yung gusto kong makasama sa mga susunod na taon. Gusto ko pa ng maraming memories with you. Gusto ko pa ng maraming adventures kasama ka at ang mga bata. Gusto kitang makasama habang tumatanda tayo, kahit makulit na tayo pareho at mas madalas na tayong magreklamo sa sakit ng katawan.

And whatever happens in life, gusto kong malaman mo na nandito lang ako. Gusto kong manatili sa tabi mo hanggang sa huling hininga ko. Gusto kong sabay nating harapin kung ano man ang dumating sa buhay natin.

I will keep choosing you.

Thank you for 18 years, Mommy. Thank you for our children, for our family, for your love, for your patience, and for still being here with me after everything.

I love you so much.

Happy 18th Anniversary, Mommy.

Love, Daddy ♥
