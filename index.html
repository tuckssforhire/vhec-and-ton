A letter for Mommy

♥

Mommy,

Eighteen years of marriage. Sometimes when I think about that, I can’t believe how far we’ve come.

We started as two college students back on November 9, 2002, probably never imagining everything that life would eventually bring us. We fell in love. We grew up together. We got married. We became Mommy and Daddy. We built our own little family and created memories that I will carry with me for the rest of my life.

Our journey hasn’t always been easy. We’ve had our fights, our misunderstandings, and moments when things between us weren’t perfect. And I know there are still ways that I fall short when it comes to us.

I may not always say the right things or show you enough how important you are to me. But I want you to know something: I’m still trying.

I’m trying to become a better husband, a better partner, a better Daddy, and a better man for you and for our family.

After all these years, you are still the woman I want beside me—through the good days, the difficult days, the ordinary days, and everything still waiting for us in the future.

Thank you for the life we’ve built together. Thank you for our children. Thank you for the memories. Thank you for staying beside me as we continue figuring life out together.

And if I had the chance to go back to those college days and choose again…

I’d still choose you, Mommy. Again and again.

Happy 18th Anniversary. I love you, always.

— Daddy ♥
