Chapter one

Where it all began

November 9, 2002

Our story began long before we became sweethearts, Mommy. Before we became husband and wife—or even dreamed of becoming Mommy and Daddy—we were elementary classmates.

Grade 4 to Grade 6. We shared the same classroom, never knowing that the classmate sitting nearby would someday become the love of my life.

The PUP entrance exam. When you took the entrance exam for Polytechnic University of the Philippines – Bataan Branch, I took it too because I wanted to study wherever you would be. I passed. You deliberately failed because you didn’t want to study there—and just like that, my clever plan completely failed. 😂

Our separate high-school days. We went to different schools and lived separate lives for a while. But during our fourth year, the route to your school passed through our road, giving me the chance to see you, talk to you, and finally court you.

The competition. I wasn’t your only suitor. There were two of us hoping you would choose one of us—but I waited, kept trying, and continued showing you how I felt.

November 9, 2002. You finally accepted my love. From that day on, our journey truly began.

From elementary classmates, to two teenagers who found each other again, to Mommy and Daddy… I’m still thankful that you chose me. ♥

Chapter two

The road to “I do”

2002 → September 20, 2008

Before we could stand before everyone as husband and wife, Mommy, we first had to protect a love that almost no one was allowed to know about.

Ours was a love that learned how to wait, travel, persevere, and keep its promises. ♥

Our secret. Your parents did not allow you to have a boyfriend until you graduated from college, so for years we kept our relationship hidden from them. It was not always easy, but we found a way to stay together.

Every Friday. You studied at Tomas del Rosario College in Balanga City, while home was more than 40 kilometers away in Mariveles. Every Friday, when you were coming home for the weekend, I would fetch you from school. That long road became part of our love story—one trip at a time, one weekend at a time.

The one time I let you go. There was a time when we broke up. Leaving you was a mistake I made only once, because I realized that the life I wanted was still the life with you in it. I returned, and we chose to keep going together.

When the secret came out. Eventually, your father learned about us. You stood up for me and defended our relationship. I also faced him myself and promised that our love would not destroy your future—that we would both finish college and take our responsibilities seriously.

A promise kept. We did what we said we would do. We persevered through the distance, the secrecy, and all the difficulties. We both graduated from college, proving that loving each other did not have to mean giving up our dreams.

Our sudden wedding. By then, our relationship had grown deeper and more intimate. When your period was delayed, a visit to the OB-GYN brought the truth into the open. Our parents knew that marriage was where our journey was already heading, so everything happened sooner and more suddenly than we had expected.

September 20, 2008. The wedding may have come together quickly, but the love behind it was not sudden at all. It had already survived six years of waiting, traveling, hiding, mistakes, forgiveness, courage, and promises kept. That was the day our secret love finally became our forever. ♥

A number only Mommy would know…

Mommy,

Eighteen years of marriage. Sometimes when I think about that, I can’t believe how far we’ve come.

We started as two college students back on November 9, 2002, probably never imagining everything that life would eventually bring us. We fell in love. We grew up together. We got married. We became Mommy and Daddy. We built our own little family and created memories that I will carry with me for the rest of my life.

Our journey hasn’t always been easy. We’ve had our fights, our misunderstandings, and moments when things between us weren’t perfect. And I know there are still ways that I fall short when it comes to us.

I may not always say the right things or show you enough how important you are to me. But I want you to know something: I’m still trying.

I’m trying to become a better husband, a better partner, a better Daddy, and a better man for you and for our family.

After all these years, you are still the woman I want beside me—through the good days, the difficult days, the ordinary days, and everything still waiting for us in the future.

Thank you for the life we’ve built together. Thank you for our children. Thank you for the memories. Thank you for staying beside me as we continue figuring life out together.

And if I had the chance to go back to those college days and choose again…

I’d still choose you, Mommy. Again and again.

Happy 18th Anniversary. I love you, always.

— Daddy ♥
